# Vedic Maths, Step by Step: a lecture course (Hindi and English)

Long lessons (8 to 12 minutes) at the chalkboard, in the lecture format: the teacher's voice, each step written up in chalk
as it is said, the answer underlined and ticked. Every lesson has the same shape: the sutra and what it says, why it works
(the simple algebra behind it, shown on the board), three or four worked examples that get harder, the common mistake, a
practice question with a pause to try it, and the answer. The Shorts stay as quick one-trick reminders that point to the
full lesson. All worked examples are computed by the program (lessons.py), never typed by hand, so the arithmetic is right.

Honest history, said plainly in Lesson 1: the sixteen sutras were published by Swami Bharati Krishna Tirtha (1884-1960) in
the book Vedic Mathematics (1965). He said they came from an appendix (parishishta) of the Atharvaveda; scholars have not
found them in any known Vedic text. The methods themselves are sound arithmetic, and each lesson shows why.

Every lesson also has a "Where you'll use it" part: the method on an everyday problem (a shop bill, prices, sharing a cost, a discount, a timetable, an exam question), computed and checked like every other example.
