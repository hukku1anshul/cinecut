        Netflix / Prime Watch Guide & TV Player

        How this works: CineCut does not copy or download anything from Netflix or Prime Video. Their video is DRM-protected, and copying it breaks their terms and anti-circumvention law. Instead, CineCut builds a watch guide: which timestamps to jump to so you watch the condensed story inside the official player, with a short recap of what you skipped.

          Optional: on-screen guide inside Netflix / Prime Video

          The CineCut Watch Guide browser extension shows the guide as a small panel on the Netflix or Prime Video page and can jump the official player to each key scene (and read the recap aloud with your computer's built-in voice). It never touches the video stream.

          1. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
          2. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder cine_cut\browser_extension.
          3. Keep CineCut running, start a film on Netflix or Prime Video, and click the CineCut button that appears.

          Seeking inside Netflix and Prime Video relies on their web players and can break when they change them. Automated control of their players may conflict with their terms of use; you use it at your own discretion.
